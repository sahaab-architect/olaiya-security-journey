# Building in Public: Olaiya's Security Journey

**From the foundations to cloud and AI security — one honest lesson at a time.**

I'm Ismail Adekunle Olaiya, a business strategist and educator developing my technical skills in cloud security and AI security.

This journal follows the work behind that journey: the concepts I learn, the problems I encounter, the assumptions I question, and the results I test.

My aim is to turn real learning experiences into useful stories for people building their own technical foundations.

## What to expect

- Weekly episodes built around real learning experiences
- Technical concepts explained in plain language
- Practical lessons from troubleshooting and lab work
- Selected evidence supporting what I have completed
- Honest accounts of what I am still learning

## How I work

I keep daily notes privately and develop selected experiences into weekly episodes.

Each published story aims to answer three questions:

1. What happened?
2. What did I learn?
3. How could that lesson help someone else?

## My commitment

I distinguish completed work from plans, and tested results from assumptions.

This journal documents skills as I develop them. Claims about my work should be supported by the evidence I share.
